## Anti-Patterns Verdict

The active game does not read as generic AI slop. Its muted arcade palette and word tiles are coherent, but the centered desktop panel is too small for the available viewport and the framing feels more assembled than authored.

The deterministic CLI scan reported one `side-tab` warning in `src/styles/theme.css` at the dormant legacy instruction style. This is a false positive in the rendered theme because the active rule overrides that border. Browser overlays in the fresh `[Human]` tab reported `nested cards` and a decorative two-axis grid background; those are valid signals for simplifying the start composition.

## Overall Impression

The core typing loop is clear, responsive, bilingual, and unusually thoughtful on Android. The largest opportunity is not another visual redesign: it is making difficulty fair and legible, giving players control during a run, and creating a reason to replay.

## What's Working

- Prefix targeting prioritizes the lowest matching word, which supports fast play once learned.
- Android keyboard handling keeps the arena stable and moves only the input.
- Word tiles, matched-character color, particles, combo, lives, and score establish a readable arcade vocabulary.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Difficulty does not govern word complexity
The English pool flattens Easy through Expert and Romanian uses one flat pool. Easy can spawn expert-length words. Keep language pools grouped by length/tier and have `DifficultyConfig` choose an allowed range.

### [P1] Escalation becomes invisible after Expert
A Hard start reaches the displayed Expert tier after 10 seconds, while mechanics continue scaling forever under the same label. Show numeric level plus a 10-second progress indicator, introduce plateaus, and define a target session length.

### [P1] Players cannot pause, leave, or change settings
Gameplay has no controls and game over offers only Play Again. Add pause/resume, restart, and home/settings actions; keep accidental activation safe with keyboard shortcuts and clear state.

### [P2] Replay feedback has no context
The result screen reports raw totals but no accuracy, WPM, personal best, or comparison by language/difficulty. Store local bests per mode and lead with one meaningful improvement statement.

### [P2] Onboarding and miss feedback are implicit
The initial field is blank for roughly 2.2-2.5 seconds, closest-word targeting is unexplained, and a miss silently applies life/combo/score consequences. Spawn the first word immediately, teach targeting in one live example, and show concise miss feedback.

## Persona Red Flags

**First-time player**: Must choose among four undefined difficulties, then remember instructions that disappear. The blank opening seconds can read as a failed start, and closest-word targeting is learned through mistakes.

**Mobile player**: Keyboard behavior is strong, but mobile hides instructions and provides no pause for interruptions. Small HUD labels and canvas-only words limit accessibility.

**Skilled repeat player**: Infinite predictable scaling, no personal best by mode, no challenge variants, and no post-run comparison make score chasing shallow after a few sessions.

## Minor Observations

- The desktop start surface is visually too small relative to the viewport.
- HUD labels are only 10px and canvas words have no semantic alternative.
- Multiple words crossing together can remove multiple lives in one frame.
- Dormant neon-era CSS increases detector noise and future styling risk.
- The input relies on placeholder text instead of a durable accessible label.

## Questions to Consider

- Is the intended run two minutes, five minutes, or endless? The curve should be designed backward from that answer.
- Should difficulty primarily mean typing complexity, falling pressure, or both?
- Is this a reflex arcade game or a typing-improvement game? Accuracy/WPM and progression differ by answer.
- What should make the second run meaningfully different from the first?
